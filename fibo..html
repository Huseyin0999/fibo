Last updated: September 24, 2026

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms of service. Users should review the privacy policies of those services.

5. Data Storage

The App does not maintain a personal database or server for storing users' personal information.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Although the App does not collect personal information, reasonable measures are taken to help ensure that the App operates securely.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the App's Google Play Store listing.
